01
Opener
~30 sec
Say exactly this
"Hey [name], thanks for the few minutes. I'm researching how pest control businesses in Dubai handle customer communication. I've got about 8 questions — totally informal, no pitch. I just want to understand how things work for you today. Cool?"
02
Context
~1 min
01
How big is the operation — roughly how many jobs a month, and who handles customer messages day-to-day?
Context
Two-in-one to move fast. Tells you their scale and whether you're talking to the decision maker or a gatekeeper.
03
Pain — Missed Leads & Response
~3 min
02
When a new customer messages you on WhatsApp at 9pm or on a Friday — what actually happens to that message?
Pain
The most revealing question. Silence = lost leads. Let them describe it — don't fill the gap for them.
03
Has a customer ever gone to a competitor because you were slow to respond — or do you know of jobs you've lost that way?
Pain
Quantifies the pain. One clear example is enough. "Happens all the time" = your ROI hook.
04
After a job is done — do you follow up with the customer at all? Ask for a Google review, check if they're happy, anything like that?
Pain
Almost nobody does this systematically. Reveals both the review opportunity and the retention gap in one answer.
05
Do you have any annual maintenance contracts with commercial clients — and when they're up for renewal, how do you currently handle that?
Signal
If they have AMCs, this is the biggest upsell angle. Manual renewals = a quantifiable revenue leak that often outweighs the chatbot value.
04
Tech & Fit
~1 min
06
Are you using WhatsApp Business or a personal WhatsApp number for the business right now — and do you have a website?
Signal
Critical qualifier. Personal WA = migration step needed. No website = the landing page is an easy win to pitch. Shapes your tier recommendation.
05
Temperature Check — The Pitch
~3 min
Transition line
"Based on what you've told me, I want to share what we're building in one sentence and get your honest reaction — takes 30 seconds."
The pitch — say this word for word
"We build a complete revenue system for pest control businesses in Dubai. Think of it this way — right now you're probably losing leads because no one replies fast enough, losing reviews because no one asks, and losing contracts because renewals slip. We fix all three: a WhatsApp bot that replies to every customer instantly, handles bookings and AMC renewals automatically — a dashboard where you can see every lead, every job, your close rate, basically your whole business on one screen — and if you don't have a website, we build you one that shows live Google reviews and converts visitors into calls. Does that sound like something your business actually needs, or are you already on top of all that?"
07
Which part of that lands hardest for you — is it the leads, the reviews, the AMC renewals, the dashboard, or the website?
Close signal
Forces them to tell you your own strongest hook for this specific customer. Whatever they pick — that's what you demo first.
08
What's your gut hesitation — is it that customers want a real person, you've tried something like this before, price, or something else?
Close signal
Forces them to name the objection. Pause after "or something else." The real objection is usually the last thing they say.
Quick-tap objection tracker
"Customers want a real person"
"Tried something like this before"
"Too expensive"
"Need to think about it"
"My customers don't use WhatsApp"
"My team handles it fine"
"Not the right time"
Other — noted in Q8 answer
09
If something like this demonstrably got you more bookings and reviews — what would be a fair monthly fee in your head? Even a rough number.
Close signal
Let them anchor first. Do not say a number. "I'm not sure" = they haven't thought about budget. AED 500+ means you're in range.
10
We're running a 30-day pilot at half price with 3 businesses in Dubai this month — zero obligation after. Would you want to be one of them?
Close signal
The real close. Yes = get their number before you hang up. Maybe = ask what would make it a yes. No = ask why — it's your best research.
06
Closing Lines
If yes or maybe
"Brilliant — let me send you a quick voice note on WhatsApp summarising exactly what we'd set up for you, takes 2 minutes. What's your WhatsApp number?"
If no
"Totally fair. One last thing — is there anything about this you'd want to see before considering it, or is this just not the right time?" Then thank them and move on. Don't rescue them from a no — just understand it.
